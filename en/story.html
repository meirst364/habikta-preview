Our story

It started
on a porch.

Boutique burgers from moshav locals, in a rustic setting, far from the noise of the city. Fresh ingredients, personal attention and plenty of love.

How we got here

  1. The porch

    During Covid, Avishai made the first burgers on the porch of his home in Tushia.

  2. The cabin

    When he saw how many people nearby wanted a good burger, he bought a small cabin and set it up beside his parents’ house.

  3. The war

    During the war he closed for three months. After a call from four hungry reservists who said they had nothing to eat, he decided to carry on, running everything on his own.

  4. Today

    A wooden cabin, tables outside, good music and hot food. We also host groups, tours and companies.

The wall

The cabin walls are covered in stickers, banknotes, license plates and tin signs, so there is always something to look at while you wait for your burger.
