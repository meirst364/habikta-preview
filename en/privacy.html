Privacy notice

What information this site receives and what happens to it.

Draft prepared by the site builders and not reviewed by a lawyer. Updated: 15 Nov 2026

Who we are

HaBikta - Moshav Burgers, 21 Derech Arvei Nachal, Tushia. Phone and WhatsApp: 058-463-3555.

What this site collects

The site itself does not store information about you. It has no user accounts, analytics tools, marketing pixels or cookies.

Table and event request forms

If you fill in a form, the details (name, date, time, number of guests, event type and notes) are only used to prepare a WhatsApp message in your own browser. None of it is sent to this site’s server. The message reaches HaBikta only if you press send in WhatsApp, and is then handled by HaBikta and by WhatsApp under its own terms. Filling in a form is optional, you can also call us.

HaBikta uses these details only to arrange your table or event and to reply to you, and not for marketing messages.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so a visitor’s IP address is sent to Google when the fonts load. Links to Google Maps, Waze, Instagram and WhatsApp lead to third-party sites with their own privacy policies.

Your rights

WhatsApp messages you send us are kept on the business phone. To view, correct or delete details you gave us, contact us by phone or WhatsApp: 058-463-3555.
