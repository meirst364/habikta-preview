Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to see the menu and request a table.

Draft prepared by the site builders and not reviewed by a lawyer. Updated: 15 Nov 2026

What we did

  • Semantic HTML and an ordered heading structure on every page
  • Full keyboard navigation, a visible focus outline and a skip-to-content link
  • Labels on every form field and error messages announced to assistive technology
  • Colour contrast of 4.5:1 or higher on the main text, by our own calculation
  • Alternative text on images, and language and direction set on every page (Hebrew right to left, English left to right)
  • Respect for the operating system “reduce motion” setting

What has not been checked yet

  • The site has not yet been tested with a real screen reader or externally audited
  • We do not claim conformance with Israeli Standard 5568 or WCAG
  • Links to external services (maps, Waze, WhatsApp, Instagram) are outside our control

Found a problem?

If something on the site is not accessible to you, contact us by phone or WhatsApp and we will help or provide the service another way, for example by phone: 058-463-3555.
